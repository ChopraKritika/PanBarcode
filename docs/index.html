    How it worksFrom DNA of many strains to a graph, anchors and a barcode.
    Where it helpsDiagnostic labs, seed testing and surveillance, for any microbe with genomes.

      Kritika Chopra

      Many genomes, one graph, and one question: what tells these strains apart? I love puzzles like that, and I love explaining them in creative ways. I’m a computational biologist finishing my master’s in Plant Pathology at UW–Madison. I spend my days between code and biology, and I enjoy making both make sense to people outside either world.

      Beyond my research, I teach coding workshops for researchers, run social media for plant-health outreach, and am turning the plant disease triangle into a board game, because good science should be fun to share.
